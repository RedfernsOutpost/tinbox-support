<h1 align="center">Tinbox</h1>

<p align="center"><b>A pocket tin full of games.</b><br>
GBA, GB and GBC on your iPhone, with no ads, no accounts and no paid version.</p>

<p align="center"><a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/id6809113803"><img src="https://toolbox.marketingtools.apple.com/api/v2/badges/download-on-the-app-store/black/en-us" alt="Download on the App Store" height="48"></a></p>

This repo holds the support page, privacy policy and issue tracker for **Tinbox Emulator** (iPhone, iOS 16 or later).

- Support page: https://redfernsoutpost.com/tinbox/
- Privacy policy: https://redfernsoutpost.com/tinbox/privacy/
- Report a problem or request a feature: [open an issue](../../issues/new).
- Homebrew Shelf catalogue (the Library > Shelf tab in the app): see [shelf/README.md](shelf/README.md) and [shelf/SOURCES.md](shelf/SOURCES.md).
- The small build patch Tinbox applies to the open-source [mGBA](https://mgba.io) core (MPL-2.0): [mgba/](mgba/README.md).
